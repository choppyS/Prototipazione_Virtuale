Virtual Prototyping & Mechatronic Simulation
This repository contains a series of exercises and projects developed during the Virtual Prototyping course of my Master's Degree in Automation Engineering.

The main goal of these projects is to bridge the gap between mechanical design and control systems, simulating the dynamic behavior of complex systems before physical manufacturing.

🔧 Key Activities & Exercises:
3D CAD Modeling & Assembly: Design of mechanical components and complex assemblies using CATIA V5.

Kinematic & Dynamic Analysis: Simulation of multibody systems to evaluate forces, torques, and motion profiles.

Co-Simulation (Control + Hardware): Integration of mechanical models with control logic to test system responses in simulated environments.
